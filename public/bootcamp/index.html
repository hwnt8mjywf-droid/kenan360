FAQ

Good to know

I'm a beginner. Can I join?

Yes. Every exercise has an easier and a harder version, and Kenan will help you pick the right one.

How much does it cost?

$25 for one session. There's no membership or contract, so you only pay for the Saturdays you come to.

Can I come more than one Saturday?

Of course. Book each Saturday you want to attend. After you book, tap “Book another Saturday” to add more dates.

What if I can't make it?

Use the “Reschedule or cancel” link on your confirmation to move or cancel your spot, or text Kenan at (848) 350-4771.
